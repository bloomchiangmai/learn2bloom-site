About the space

Designed around how children naturally move and gather.

Bloom Space was chosen because it reflects what we believe about learning. Children need room to move, quiet corners to think, open courtyards to gather, and an environment that feels genuinely calm and alive.

The space includes dedicated learning zones for each age group, a sheltered courtyard for outdoor learning, a making and project studio, quiet reading areas, and shared community spaces for group work and family gatherings.

Location

Based at Bloom Space, Hang Dong.

Bloom Chiangmai is housed within Bloom Space, a purpose-built creative learning environment in Hang Dong, a quiet green district about 15 minutes south of Chiang Mai city. Bloom Space was chosen because it already reflects what Bloom Chiangmai believes about learning: open, warm, and built for children.

Hub community

A place where families feel at home too.

The Hub is open to families, not just learners. We run open mornings each term so prospective and current families can visit, meet the coaches, and see learning in action.

At the end of each term, learners share their work at Community Day. Families connect, projects get celebrated, and the Hub marks what has been built together over the term.
