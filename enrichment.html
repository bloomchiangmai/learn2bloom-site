About enrichment at Bloom Chiangmai

More to explore, more to create.

The learning day at Bloom Chiangmai runs from 10:00 to 16:00. But learning doesn't stop there. Through our partnership with Bloom Space, Bloom Chiangmai families have access to a full programme of after-school activities and weekend camps, covering art, music, dance, and more.

These programmes are run by Bloom Space and are open to all children in the community, not just Bloom Chiangmai learners. They're designed to complement the Bloom Chiangmai day, providing creative and social outlets in a familiar, trusted environment.

In partnership with

Bloom Space, Hang Dong

A creative community space offering classes and workshops in art, music, dance, and beyond for all ages.

Bloom Chiangmai families — 15% off all programmes

Regular programmes

After school and weekends.

Two ongoing programme formats run throughout the academic year. Enrolment is open to Bloom Chiangmai learners and the wider community.

After-School Programme

Monday – Friday · 16:00 – 18:00

A structured but relaxed after-school session for children who want to keep creating after the Bloom Chiangmai day ends. Activities rotate across art, music, movement, and guided creative projects.

Holiday camps

Something for every break.

Bloom Space runs holiday camps during school breaks throughout the year. Details and dates are announced each term.

Term break camps

January & February

Short intensive camps during the break between Term 1 and Term 2. Creative and active programming for all age groups.

Songkran camp

April

A special camp around the Thai New Year. Cultural activities, outdoor programming, and community celebration.

Summer camp

July & August

The biggest camp of the year. Multi-week programmes across art, music, movement, and creative exploration.
