About the space

Designed around how learners naturally move and gather.

This space was chosen because it reflects what we believe about learning. Learners need room to move, quiet corners to think, open courtyards to gather, and an environment that feels genuinely calm and alive.

The space includes open learning areas for each age group, a garden and outdoor play space, and quiet corners for reading and calm time throughout the day. In total, the campus spans 3,000 sqm indoors and out.

Location

Tuition includes snacks. Lunch is optional, 100 THB per day, billed monthly based on attendance. Dietary requirements accommodated.

On-site parking available. No bus service currently. Families arrange their own transport or carpool.

Dress code

No uniform. Comfortable, practical clothing suitable for indoor and outdoor learning.

Hub community

A place where families feel at home too.

The Hub is open to families, not just learners. We run open mornings each term so prospective and current families can visit, meet the coaches, and see learning in action.

At the end of each term, learners share their work at Community Day. Families connect, projects get celebrated, and the Hub marks what has been built together over the term.
