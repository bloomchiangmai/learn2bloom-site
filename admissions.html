How to apply

Five steps from enquiry to offer.

The application process is designed to be straightforward. Most families complete it within two to three weeks.

01

Enquire

Submit your interest via the form below or message us on WhatsApp. We will respond within 24 hours.

02

Visit

Come and see the Hub. Meet the coaches, see the space, and ask everything you want to know.

03

Apply

Complete the application form. We will ask about your child's background, learning style, and what you are looking for.

04

Meeting

A relaxed conversation between your family, your child, and our lead coach. Not a test, a chance to get to know each other.

05

Offer

If it is a good fit for both sides, we extend a formal offer. Enrollment is confirmed on receipt of the enrollment fee and deposit.

Admissions FAQ

Questions about the process.

If your question is not here, message us directly on WhatsApp or Line.

How long does the application process take?

Most families complete the process in two to three weeks from initial enquiry to offer. We try to move quickly, we know families are planning ahead and need certainty.

What does the family meeting involve?

It is a relaxed conversation, not a test or assessment. We want to understand your child, your family's values, and what you are hoping for. Your child is welcome to come along, we find it helpful to meet them in an informal setting before they start.

Can siblings apply at the same time?

Yes, and we encourage it. Siblings can apply together and attend different zones at the same Hub. A 10% sibling discount applies to the monthly tuition of the second and subsequent children.

What if my child has special learning needs?

We assess each child individually. The zone-based, coach-led model at Bloom Chiangmai is well-suited to children who learn differently, but we are honest about what we can and cannot support. We will discuss this openly during the application process.

Is the enrollment fee refundable if we change our minds?

The enrollment fee of ฿ 20,000 is non-refundable once paid. The refundable deposit (one month's tuition) is returned in full on exit with adequate notice.

Can we join mid-year?

Mid-year enrollment is considered on a case-by-case basis subject to available places. Contact us directly to discuss your situation.
