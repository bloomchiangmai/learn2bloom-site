Our approach

Educating both the Heart and the Mind.

Most schools were designed for a different era. Bloom Chiangmai was built around how children actually develop, with the environment, the people, and the structure to match.

The quality of the adult determines the quality of the relationship to the child. The quality of the relationship to the child has a profound impact on the child's ability to learn. That's why personal and professional development are the cornerstones of Heart-Mind Education.

Mission

We develop the inner coherence of the adults who shape children's environments, because no learning environment can hold more wisdom than the adults who built it.

Vision

Children raised inside Heart-Mind environments grow into adults who bring self-awareness, responsibility, and compassion into the families, classrooms, and workplaces they go on to shape.

How we think about learning

Seven layers.
One learner.

Bloom Chiangmai is built from seven interconnected layers, each one supporting everything above it. Philosophy is the foundation at the bottom. University and life pathways are the outcome at the top.

D1  The Mind  ·  Attention & Presence; Curiosity & Inquiry; Cognitive Flexibility; Meaning-Making
D2  The Heart  ·  Self-Awareness; Emotional Regulation; Empathy & Perspective Taking; Ethical Awareness
D3  Embodied Adventure  ·  Effort & Persistence; Relationship to Challenge; Agency and Responsibility; Transfer & Application
D4  Integrated Capacity  ·  Awareness in Action; Alignment with Values

Heart-Mind Education nurtures the development of 14 Universal Learning Standards that are the foundation of a balanced life that benefits both the individual and the community around them. Embodying those standards leads to what every adult needs in a fast-changing world:

A deep love of learning because the knowledge and skills mastered now might not be enough in a year.

Sharp learning skills, because efficiency builds self-confidence.

An appetite for challenges, because they're bound to show up.

A child who faces challenges with a smile and is willing to and capable of working hard does not fear the future, however uncertain it is.

The people

Learning Coaches, not teachers.

Our coaches do more than deliver curriculum. They guide, support, challenge, and genuinely accompany each learner through their development journey.

The adults at Bloom Chiangmai play two deliberately separate roles. No one stands at a board delivering a lesson to rows of children.

Academic Learning

Deliver the curriculum, track academic progress, and provide subject expertise across all zones.

  • Subject knowledge and curriculum delivery
  • Academic progress tracking
  • UK and US pathway guidance
  • Assessment and evaluation
Nurturing the mind. Focused on what your child knows and can do

Learning coaches

Support each child's inner life, motivation, planning, reflection, and emotional development.

  • Developing agency: whether self-appointed or mandated by the learning coach, the child chooses to commit to the challenge at hand and to assume responsibility for the outcomes
  • Building competence: long term goals set the direction of effort; day-to-day tasks are monitored for progress made in the right direction
  • Engaging with purpose: the pride we get from trying our best is the first purpose attached to each challenge
Nurturing the heart. Focused on who your child is becoming

We believe wellbeing and academic rigour should strengthen each other, not compete with each other.

Learning Rhythm

Balanced by design.
Not by accident.

The daily structure at Bloom Chiangmai is deliberate. Half the day is focused academic learning. A quarter is applied, projects, making, experimenting, creating. The final quarter is holistic, movement, outdoor learning, artistic work, and social development.

Approximately 3 hours of focused academic learning per day across a 10-month academic year.

Inner development

Learning shaped by more than knowledge.

How a child manages their attention, understands their feelings, and relates to others is as important as what they know. We build time for this into every day, because it is the foundation everything else rests on.

This is not a separate programme. It is woven into how the day is structured, how coaches relate to children, and how the environment itself is designed.

AI-assisted learning

Technology that supports.
People who guide.

Independent use of an electronic device starts in upper primary after a rigorous rite of passage. Learners at that level still have limited time on a computer. Secondary learners use AI tools to support personalised pacing, practice, and progress tracking. The insights they generate are interpreted by the coaches who know your child.

Technology supports learning, it never replaces the people in the room. Every data point passes through a human relationship before it becomes a decision.
